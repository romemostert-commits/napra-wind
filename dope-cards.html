Wind Dope Cards

Six blank cards. Print, then fill in each one at the line from the app's Stage Summary — write the Offset, Min, Recommended and Max hold for each target. Cut along the dashed guides.

NAPRA · Wind Dope Cards MILS · hold = dial into wind · L / R = wind from
How to use: One card per stage. Fill Match and Stage, tick which way the wind is from, then copy each target's Offset (dial from T1), Min / Max hold (your lightest & heaviest reads) and Recommended hold from the app. Holds in mils, e.g. 0.5 L.
